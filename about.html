Our founder

Founded by Hos Movahedi

“After studying at several Vancouver institutions, including UBC, I saw a real need for accessible, high-quality education—and a gap between experienced professionals and the learners who could benefit from their expertise. That insight inspired me to start this work.

For more than a decade, I’ve partnered with colleges across Canada to develop learning materials, design programs, and train instructors. My work has included ILAC International College, Cloud 9 College, Blanche Macdonald Centre, Oxford International Career Colleges, and others.”

Hos Movahedi Founder, CTLS
