How we work

From goal to finished program.

Three steps from your first message to delivery.

Discuss a project

1. Share your goal

Tell us the subject, your learners, and what they should be able to do.

2. We design and build

We find and verify the right professionals, then design the learning around your outcomes and create the content, workshop, or system.

3. We deliver

Our professionals teach, or we train your instructors to run it.

Example projects.

Illustrations of what we could do. These are not past client projects.

01Team development · Workshop

Stronger teamwork, led by the right expert.

A team leader wants practical teamwork training. We could offer three organizational behaviour consultants to choose from, each with a business degree and verified CPHR credentials.

Once they choose, we could design the training kit around practical teamwork activities and lead a five-hour workshop.

02International education · Curriculum

A full business curriculum, ready to teach.

A college in India wants a business curriculum inspired by Vancouver programs. We could design the curriculum around clear learning outcomes, write all course content, and build it in its learning platform, such as Moodle, Canvas, or Google Classroom.

We could also train its instructors and provide the structure and guidance to run it.

03Program design · Hybrid learning

Hybrid programs designed for real learners.

A Vancouver college wants hybrid programs and materials ready for PTIB review. We could review its requirements and students, then work with verified professionals on in-person and online classes.

Each program could be designed around clear outcomes and include full learning materials, assessments, and step-by-step instructor guidance.

04International trade · Sales training

Import logistics training built on real workflows.

A company wants its Chilean sales team trained on import logistics and the USMCA. We could send an MBA-qualified Canadian consultant and build training around import workflows, documentation, shipping, and how the USMCA applies to eligible trade between Canada, the U.S., and Mexico.

Scenario-based practice and job aids could match the team’s sales situations and flag when to consult customs experts.

05Investment education · Risk management

Risk workshops for major real estate projects.

Investors in Shenzhen want a workshop on financial risk for large real estate projects. We could bring a Toronto-based CFA charterholder from an investment fund to cover project assumptions, financing exposure, cash-flow sensitivity, and downside scenarios.

Case exercises could help participants challenge assumptions and compare risks.

06LMS implementation · Ongoing support

A connected learning system for your college.

A college needs a custom LMS, a website, and a secure student database, with monthly maintenance.

We could plan and build the system, structure each course inside it, provide technical, administrator, and user documentation for handover, and maintain it every month.
