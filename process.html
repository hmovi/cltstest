How we work

From learning goals to programs that work.

These illustrative scenarios show how CTLS could bring the right people, relevant materials, and clear delivery plans to projects of different scales.

Discuss a project

01Team development · Workshop

Stronger teamwork starts with the right expertise.

Imagine Dani reaching out for practical training to help her organization work better as a team. We could introduce her to three organizational behaviour consultants to choose from, each with a business degree and verified CPHR credentials.

If she selected a consultant, we could build a custom training kit, design the workshop, and lead a five-hour session for her staff.

02International education · Curriculum

A full business curriculum, ready to teach.

A college in India might be looking for a complete business curriculum inspired by programs at colleges and universities in Vancouver. We could develop all relevant course content and build the program in its chosen learning platform, such as Moodle, Canvas, or Google Classroom.

To make it turnkey, we could train the instructors and provide the materials, structure, and implementation guidance needed to run the program.

03Program design · Hybrid learning

Hybrid programs designed for real learners.

A Vancouver college might want to add hybrid programs and prepare materials for a PTIB review. We could review its requirements and student body, then work with verified professionals to develop a mix of in-person and dynamic online classes.

Each program could include complete learning materials and step-by-step instructor guidance to support consistent delivery and the college’s review process.

04International trade · Sales training

Import logistics training, built around real workflows.

A company might bring an MBA-qualified consultant from Canada to Chile to train its sales team on import logistics and the USMCA. We could build practical training around import workflows, documentation, shipping, and how the USMCA applies to eligible trade among Canada, the U.S., and Mexico.

Sessions and job aids could be tailored to the team’s sales scenarios, with guidance on when to consult customs experts for compliance decisions.
