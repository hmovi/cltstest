How we work

From learning goals to programs that work.

From a focused staff workshop to a complete curriculum, we bring the right people, materials, and delivery plan together.

Discuss a project

01Team development · Workshop

Stronger teamwork starts with the right expertise.

Dani wanted practical training to help her organization work better as a team. We introduced her to three organizational behaviour consultants, each with a business degree and verified CPHR credentials.

After she chose a consultant, we created a custom training kit, designed the workshop, and delivered a five-hour session for her staff.

02International education · Curriculum

A full business curriculum, ready to teach.

A college in India wanted a business curriculum inspired by programs at colleges and universities in Vancouver. We developed the complete program in its chosen learning platform, such as Moodle, Canvas, or Google Classroom.

Then we trained the instructors and handed over a turnkey curriculum, ready for delivery.

03Program design · Hybrid learning

Hybrid programs designed for real learners.

A Vancouver college wanted to add hybrid programs and prepare for a PTIB review. We assessed the requirements and learner needs, then worked with verified professionals to develop a balanced mix of in-person and dynamic online classes.

Each program came with complete learning materials and step-by-step instructor guidance for delivery.
